Colophon & Bibliography

This book explores the parallels between metallic corrosion and human degradation.

While one text examines how humans are agents of their own corrosion, the other examines how metals corrode and how we may prevent their degradation through caring for them.

The two texts run parallel to each other while also directionally, and thematically, opposing each other.
